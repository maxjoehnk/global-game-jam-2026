# Maske kann auch geil sein

In this game you are former german minister of health Jens Spahn and want to get rid of all evidence of your wrongdoing in the covid pandemic. It's a platforming game with a strong thematic focus, you can switch between different layers of each level, collect items to fight your political opponents and save yourself from some hazards. All to get to your well deserved bribes.

Download on [Global Game Jam Site](https://globalgamejam.org/games/2026/maske-kann-auch-geil-sein-3)
